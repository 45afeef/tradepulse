# Your account and security

## Signing in and out

You stay signed in on a device until you sign out. For safety the app renews your access automatically in the background; you will not notice it. **Sign out** ends the session on this device. **Sign out everywhere** ends every session on every device immediately.

## Changing your password

Open **Settings** and use **Change password**. You need your current password, and the new one must be at least 10 characters. Changing it signs out every other device.

## If you forget your password

Ask your operator. They can generate a temporary password, shown to them once, and you are signed out everywhere. Sign in with it and change it right away under **Settings**.

## Too many failed attempts

After 10 failed sign-in attempts for an account it is locked for about 15 minutes. This protects you from guessing attacks, and it applies the same way whether or not the account exists.

## Suspended accounts

An operator can suspend an account. A suspended account is signed out, receives no alerts and cannot sign in until it is reactivated.

## Appearance

Under **Settings** choose light, dark or follow your device.
