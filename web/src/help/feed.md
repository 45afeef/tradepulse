# Your feed

The feed shows everything that matches what you follow, newest first, and updates live while you watch.

## Reading items

Each card shows where the item came from, its topics, how long ago it arrived and, when it matters, a **Notable** or **High** badge. The title opens the original page in a new tab. Select **Why this** to see the reason the item reached you, for example that you follow its source or a keyword it contains.

## New items while you read

At the top of the page new items appear on their own. If you have scrolled down, a button such as **3 new items** appears instead, so the page never jumps under your finger. Select it to jump to the top.

If your connection drops, the indicator changes to **Offline, retrying**. When it comes back the app fetches what you missed, so nothing is lost.

## Views {#views}

A **view** is a saved filter for your feed. Select **+ View**, give it a name and choose any of:

- **Sources**: items from any of these sources.
- **Topics**: items about any of these topics.
- **Keywords**: whole words or phrases in the title or text.
- **Time window**: only items from the last hour, day, week and so on.

Everything you set must match, and within one list any value matches. For example, source CNBC plus topic markets shows only CNBC items about markets. You can keep up to 20 saved views, and each appears as a tab above the feed. Views only narrow what is already in your feed; they never change what you receive.

> **Note:** Views search the most recent 1,000 items in your feed.

## Rating items

**Useful** and **Not useful** are private to you. They help the operators see which sources and alerts are noisy, as anonymous totals. Select the same button again to undo it.

## Muting

Under [Following](/following) you can add muted words. Items mentioning them are hidden from your feed.
