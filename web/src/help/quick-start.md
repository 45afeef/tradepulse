# Getting started

TradePulse collects what you care about from many places into one live feed, and alerts you the moment something important happens. This page gets you from invite to first alert in about two minutes.

## 1. Join with your invite

TradePulse is invite-only. Open the link you were sent (or go to the sign-up page and paste your invite code), enter your email and choose a password of at least 10 characters. A few random words make a strong password.

> **Tip:** An invite works once and expires. If it says it is used, expired or meant for another email, ask the person who invited you for a new one.

## 2. Tell us what to follow

The first screen asks what you want to follow. You can pick any mix of:

- **Sources**: whole publishers or systems, for example a news site. You get everything they publish.
- **Topics**: tags on items, for example markets or outages.
- **Keywords**: words or phrases that appear in titles and text.

You can change all of this later under **Following**.

## 3. Choose your alerts

Alerts are for the moments you cannot afford to miss. Pick a ready-made template and add the words or topics to watch. Each template shows a **preview**: how often it would have fired recently, so you can avoid noise before you start. More in [Alerts](/help/alerts).

## 4. Use your feed

Your [feed](/help/feed) updates live. The small indicator at the top shows **Live** when you are connected. New items slide in at the top; if you have scrolled down, a button tells you how many are waiting.

## What next

- Tap **Useful** or **Not useful** on items and alerts. It keeps your feed sharp and tells the operators which sources are worth keeping.
- Save a [view](/help/feed#views) for a topic you check often.
- Send alerts to your own tools with a [webhook](/help/delivery).
- Stuck? See [Questions and troubleshooting](/help/faq).
