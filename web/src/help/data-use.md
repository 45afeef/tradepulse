# How your data is used

This is the beta version of our data notice, written to describe what the system really does. Your operator is responsible for how this workspace is run and can answer questions.

## What is stored about you

- **Account:** your email, a protected form of your password (never the password itself), your role and status, and when you joined.
- **Your choices:** what you follow, muted words, saved views, alert rules and webhook addresses.
- **Your feed:** the items delivered to you, keeping your most recent 1,000.
- **Alert history and delivery log:** kept for 30 days.
- **Ratings:** your useful and not useful marks, kept for one year.
- **Sessions:** sign-in tokens are stored in a protected form and removed within a week after they expire.
- **Technical logs:** the servers record each request with its time, path, result, a reference id and the network address it came from, to keep the service secure and working. How long these logs are kept depends on the hosting provider.

## What operators can see

Operators can see your email, role and status, and a log of operator actions (such as inviting or suspending someone), kept for one year. Totals of ratings are shown without names. The app does not show operators your feed, what you follow or your alerts. Whoever hosts the database can technically access stored data, which is why access to it is limited to the people running the service.

## What we do not do

- No advertising and no selling or sharing of your data.
- The web app contains no analytics or advertising trackers.
- Webhook secrets are shown to you once and are not stored in readable form.

## Webhooks leave the system

When you add a webhook, alerts are sent to the address you chose. What happens to them there is up to you and whoever runs that address.

## Deleting your data

There is no self-service deletion yet. Ask your operator to remove your account and data.

## Questions

Ask your operator, the person who invited you.
