# Questions and troubleshooting

## My feed is empty

You may not follow anything yet: open **Following** or the **Get started** page and pick sources, topics or keywords. If you do follow things, new items appear as soon as the sources publish them; some sources are checked every few minutes.

## I am not getting alerts

1. Open **Alerts** and check the rule is switched on and not paused.
2. Check its values: a keyword must appear as a whole word in the title or text.
3. Check the cooldown: after one alert the rule stays quiet for that long.
4. Use the preview when editing: it shows whether the rule would have fired recently.
5. For webhooks, check **Delivery** for a disabled channel or failed deliveries.

## An alert was paused

It fired more than the allowed number of times in an hour. See [why an alert gets paused](/help/alerts#why-an-alert-gets-paused).

## The top bar says "Offline, retrying"

Your connection to the live stream dropped. The app reconnects by itself and fetches what you missed. If it stays offline, check your network or reload the page.

## I was signed out

Your session ended: you signed out everywhere, changed your password, or an operator reset or suspended your account. Sign in again.

## My invite code does not work

Invites work once, expire, and can be tied to one email address. Ask the person who invited you for a new one.

## A webhook was disabled

It failed 10 deliveries in a row. Fix your receiver, then select **Re-enable** under **Delivery**. See [Webhooks](/help/delivery).

## Items look different from the source website

TradePulse shows the title, a short summary and a link. Open the original for the full story. Some sources are read from their public pages or feeds, so small differences are normal.

## How do I delete my account or data?

There is no self-service deletion yet. Ask your operator. See [How your data is used](/help/data-use).

## Something else is wrong

Note what you did and, if you see one, the reference id in the error message, and send it to your operator.
